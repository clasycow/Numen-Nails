Appointment policies · Before your visit

Client Agreement

Please read these policies before requesting an appointment. They help keep every visit comfortable, clean, and on schedule.

  • No guests. Numen Nails is home-based, and only the client receiving the service may attend the appointment. Please do not bring friends, family members, or other guests.
  • No accompanying children. Children may not be brought along to someone else’s appointment. Please arrange childcare before your visit.
  • Clients must be age 11 or older. Children are welcome only when they are the scheduled client and are at least 11 years old. I do not provide services to children under 11.
  • I do not work over another nail artist’s work.
  • Please do not book while sick. If you become sick before your appointment, reschedule as soon as possible.
  • A $10 deposit is required through Cash App. Deposits are nonrefundable if you cancel.
  • Please arrive on time. A five-minute grace period is provided because other clients may be scheduled afterward.
  • Please remember I am a beginner; however, I can do lots of designs. Please be aware that longer sets with intricate designs may take anywhere from 3–5 hours. Please do not come with anything scheduled after your appointment.
  • Remaining balances are cash only.
  • Cancellations or reschedules must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.

When submitting your booking inquiry, please confirm that you have read and agree to these policies, including the guest and minimum-age requirements.
